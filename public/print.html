Master Sheet — skinny fat → strong, lean, sharp

Ground rules.
  1. Sleep 7½ h or more. Nothing else on this sheet works well without it.
  2. Eat the targets below every day. Skinny-fat bodies change only when you eat enough to build and train hard enough to need it.
  3. Train hard on A–F. Sunday (G) stays light. Never skip the 06:00 walk.
  4. Add weight or reps every week. If a set felt easy, go up next time. That is the whole programme.
  5. Weigh in every Sunday morning and take photos monthly (front, side, back, same light). Judge by 4-week trends, not days.

8 · Honest notes

  • Abs and bulk. Gaining to 66–68 kg and showing abs at the same time is hard. Expect strong shoulders, chest, back and arms first. Abs show as the waist stays lean. Do not eat less to chase abs while gaining.
  • White hair. Early greying at 27 is often genetic. It can also come from low B12, iron, ferritin or thyroid problems and from stress. Get a blood test (B12, vitamin D, ferritin, TSH) once. Existing white hairs will not turn black again.
  • Hair density. Minoxidil 5% works only while you keep using it. Expect shedding in weeks 2–8, then regrowth at 4–6 months. Use it every day without gaps.
  • Beard. Minoxidil on the beard is off-label and a decision for you to take with a doctor. This plan keeps it on the scalp only.
  • Stretch marks and dark patches. They fade slowly over months. Mon/Wed/Fri AHA, daily moisturiser, daily sun cover and patience are what work. They will not vanish fully.
  • Face puffiness. Salt, poor sleep and alcohol are the usual causes. Slim your waist and drink water. A real "lifted" jawline comes from body fat dropping and a better posture. Neck exercises help.
  • Grief and sleep. You are doing this after losing your father. Being kind to yourself on a bad day does not break the plan. Missing one session is fine; missing two in a row is the one to avoid.
